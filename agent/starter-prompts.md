# Workout Remotion Director v1.0 — Starter Prompts

These examples are intentionally natural-language requests. The Director should make routine creative decisions itself rather than requiring a technical editing brief.

## One and done

> I uploaded several workout clips. Make this social media ready. Choose the strongest truthful direction yourself, carry the edit through QA, and return one finished vertical video.

## Quick Reel / TikTok / Short

> Make these clips into a quick workout Reel. Keep it punchy, choose the hook yourself, mute the camera audio, and finish the video for me.

## Longer workout edit

> Make a longer workout video that shows more of the actual working sets. Keep the movement easy to follow and carry it through the final render.

## High-effort hook

> Open with my strongest truthful high-effort rep, then make the rest of the workout flow naturally. Choose the exact rep and pacing yourself.

The Director must treat apparent effort as visual evidence rather than proof of muscular failure.

## Instructional / coaching overlays

> Make this an instructional workout edit. Use these cues at the relevant movement phases: “Control the descent,” “Brace,” and “Drive.” Keep the text out of the way of my body and equipment.

User-provided coaching language can be timed to visible movement phases when supportable. The Director should not invent medical or rehabilitation claims.

## Rep counters

> Make this social media ready and count the reps in my final set. Only show a counter when the completed reps are clear enough to count confidently.

## Keep source audio

> Make this into a short workout video, but keep the original audio from the final set. You can mute the other clips.

This explicitly overrides the default muted-source-audio behavior for the requested scope.

## Exercise labels

> Make this one finished workout Reel and label each exercise when it starts. If you cannot confidently identify an exercise, leave that label out instead of guessing.

## A/B hook variants

> Make two versions of this workout video for an A/B test. Keep everything else as similar as practical, but use a high-effort hook in version A and a curiosity-based truthful hook in version B.

A/B variants are opt-in. Change one major hypothesis at a time.

## Revision

> Keep the edit the same, but change only the opening hook. Choose a stronger truthful opening and rerender it.

## More explicit creative brief

> Make these clips into one fast TikTok workout. Start with my hardest-looking successful rep, mute the raw camera audio, show most of my final set, count the clearly completed final-set reps in the upper left, and put each exercise name up when it starts. Keep overlays away from my joints and equipment. Use movement comprehension over beat sync. QA it and return the finished video.

Explicit instructions narrow the Director's choices but do not remove its responsibility to complete the workflow automatically.
