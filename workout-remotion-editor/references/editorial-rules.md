# Editorial rules

## Source truth and narrative integrity

- Review the full source timeline, not only thumbnails or detected highlights.
- Keep an audit trail from every selected segment to a source and time range.
- Preserve chronology unless a replay, flash-forward, montage, or reordering is clearly signaled.
- Never manufacture a repetition, result, quote, reaction, struggle, or before/after relationship.
- Slow motion, freeze frames, zooms, sound design, and repeated angles must clarify—not falsify—an event.
- Do not cut mid-repetition unless it is an intentional, comprehensible hook or transition.
- Prefer complete movement cycles and include short visual/audio handles.

## Story construction

A short edit usually needs: a truthful hook, enough context to understand it, a progression, a payoff, and an optional close. Match pacing to the platform and purpose rather than cutting mechanically on every beat. Alternate wide/context and detail views only when the available coverage supports continuity.

### Modes

**Quick:** concise assembly and minimal graphic system. **Standard:** polished arc with complete overlay/audio plan. **Extended:** selects reel, alternate structures, instructional detail, review checkpoints, and platform variants.

## Selection rubric

Score candidates only as an organizing aid: relevance to brief, movement visibility, technical quality, emotional/story value, continuity, and rights/privacy suitability. Record why a lower-quality shot is essential. Confidence labels: `high` (clear evidence), `medium` (likely with limited ambiguity), `low` (do not assert without review).

## Fitness-specific integrity

- Call an attempt successful only when the footage establishes the selected completion rule.
- Do not portray normal exertion as crisis or possible pain as motivation.
- Avoid appearance-based or shaming language.
- Coaching language must describe observable positions/actions and acknowledge camera-angle limitations.
- If form changes across the set, do not use an earlier clean rep to claim a later rep was clean.

## Paper-edit fields

For each item include: `id`, `source_id`, `source_in`, `source_out`, `timeline_in`, `timeline_out`, `purpose`, `transition`, `audio`, `speed`, linked `overlay_ids`, and `confidence`. Note crops, reframes, stabilization, replay labeling, and handles. Resolve overlaps and gaps deliberately.
